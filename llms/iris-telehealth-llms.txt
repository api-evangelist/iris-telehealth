# Iris Telehealth

> Iris Telehealth is an Austin, Texas telepsychiatry company, founded in 2013, that delivers
> virtual behavioral health care to healthcare organizations rather than direct to consumers.
> It contracts psychiatrists, psychiatric nurse practitioners, LCSWs and LPCs to hospitals and
> health systems, community mental health centers, community health centers and FQHCs, and
> Certified Community Behavioral Health Clinics.

## Agent-relevant summary

Iris Telehealth has **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API documentation, no
SDK, no CLI, no MCP server, no agent card, and no machine-readable API contract of any kind.
There is nothing here for an agent to call. This file exists so that an agent asking the
question gets a definite answer instead of crawling a marketing site looking for one.

Iris Telehealth's delivery model is deliberately technology-agnostic. Its clinicians work
inside each partner organization's existing EMR/EHR and e-prescribing tools over
HIPAA-compliant video conferencing. The integration surface therefore belongs to the
partner's systems (Epic, Cerner, athenahealth and similar), not to Iris. Engagement runs
through a sales conversation, not a signup form.

## Services

- [Virtual Clinic](https://iristelehealth.com/services/virtual-clinic/): Comprehensive care and behavioral health integration across patient journeys.
- [On-Demand Services](https://iristelehealth.com/services/on-demand-services/): 24/7 emergency department psychiatry support.
- [Scheduled Services](https://iristelehealth.com/services/scheduled-services/): Behavioral health providers matched to a healthcare organization's needs.
- [Bridge Clinic](https://iristelehealth.com/services/bridge-clinic/): Transitional behavioral health care.
- [Iris Insights](https://iristelehealth.com/services/iris-insights/): AI-enabled behavioral health analytics - risk optimization, capacity management, a behavioral health command center, and revenue cycle analytics. Delivered as a managed analytics service; no public API or data feed is documented.
